# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: prospective clients looking for a law firm and deciding whether to contact Rockfall Lekgowe Group. Other audiences (partners, procurement, job seekers) are not confirmed and are out of scope until the user says otherwise.

## Product Purpose
A company website for Rockfall Lekgowe Group, a law firm. It presents the firm to prospective clients and makes it easy to get in touch. Success means a visitor understands what the firm does, trusts it, and makes contact.

The site is currently a pitch (a proposal shown to the firm), not yet a live production site.

## Positioning
Tagline: "efficient quality and affordable legal services". The firm's claim is quality that is efficient and affordable. Which practice areas it covers, and what proves the claim, is not yet captured.

## Operating Context
Legal services. Visitors are likely weighing cost, speed, and trustworthiness before reaching out. Exact practice areas, jurisdiction, and regulatory body are undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static-first Astro site with React islands, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui (Base UI).
- Contact channels: contact form, email, phone, and WhatsApp.
- Open decisions, not to be invented: practice areas, jurisdiction, office locations, team members, fees or pricing, registrations, and legal or regulatory claims.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "Rockfall Lekgowe Group".
- Tagline: "efficient quality and affordable legal services".
- A logo exists but is not in the repo yet.
- The user has supplied a DESIGN.md (design system "Counsel") describing the visual direction. Treat it as the user's own visual authority, not something to redesign.

## Evidence on Hand
The user reports having a logo, project photos, and testimonials or named clients, but none are in the repository (`public/` only holds the default favicon). Because this is a pitch, any placeholder content must be clearly marked as placeholder. Do not present invented testimonials, client names, case results, statistics, or credentials as real.

## Product Principles
- Credibility over claims: show real, specific proof of quality rather than generic legal language.
- Make "affordable" and "efficient" concrete wherever the firm can supply facts, such as response times, fee approach, or process.
- Make contacting the firm easy, with form, email, phone, and WhatsApp reachable from every page.
- Use only facts the user has supplied; mark gaps visibly instead of filling them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No product-specific standard established. Visitors may be stressed or unfamiliar with legal language, so plain wording and clear contact paths matter.
