## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for formal, high-stakes professional services communication—including formal legal notices, executive client letters, institutional announcements, and regulatory updates.

### Tone & Personality
- **Authoritative & Measured:** Inspires immediate institutional confidence. Uses formal editorial restraint rather than conversational levity.
- **Calm & Unhurried:** Eliminates sensory friction through expansive whitespace, structured line rules, and deliberate typographic rhythm.
- **Singular Focus:** Governed by the strict principle of "One emphasis per message"—highlighting the single critical date, action, or fact while letting all supporting information remain neutral and legible.

### Design Aesthetic
The visual language merges classic British legal and publishing typography with crisp, contemporary digital execution:
- High-contrast transitional serifs for titles and section heads.
- Architectural hairline borders (1px) in place of soft shadows or heavy structural divisions.
- Lightly rounded corners (6px on controls, about 8px on panels), framed by hairlines rather than shadows.
- Left-edge vertical accent borders (3px to 4px) to anchor lead headings and critical dispatch summaries.

## Colors

The palette relies on a disciplined hierarchy of contrast and saturation. Deep Navy acts as the primary voice of authority, Burgundy serves strictly as a focused accent, and balanced neutrals ground body copy and framing.

### Functional Palette
- **Primary (Navy) (`#14295C`):** Used for primary brand markers, major serif headers, lead navigational elements, and primary buttons. Achieves a 14.0:1 contrast ratio against white.
- **Accent (Burgundy) (`#7C1022`):** Reserved for the primary point of emphasis—statutory deadlines, critical amounts, urgent status indicators, callout bar accents, and the page's important call-to-action buttons (on dark navy surfaces the lighter secondary red `#A9343F` is used so the button stays visible). Delivers a 10.7:1 contrast ratio against white. Never used purely for decorative fill.
- **Page Ground (Paper) (`#F4F5F8`):** The foundational canvas color evoking fine archival paper; softens glare and contrasts cleanly against pure white card surfaces.
- **Surface (White) (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity ground for cards, letterheads, panels, dialogs, and input backgrounds.
- **Ink (`#1A2138`):** Primary body text color, offering comfortable, high-legibility contrast (14.6:1).
- **Slate (`#4A5268`):** Secondary text, timestamps, captions, and non-critical metadata (7.1:1 on white).
- **Line (`#D3D8E3`):** 1px hairline rules, borders, table dividers, and input container edges.
- **Navy Tint (`#E3E8F3`):** Neutral informational backgrounds, subtle active states, and table headers.
- **Burgundy Tint (`#F6E4E7`):** Background fill for high-priority alert cards, deadline warnings, and flagged statements.

## Typography

Typography establishes immediate institutional authority through deliberate juxtaposition: a high-contrast editorial serif paired with a hyper-legible neutral sans-serif.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Editorial Headings (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Reserved for document titles, modal headers, legal dispatch subjects, and card titles. When paired with a vertical accent bar, the heading aligns flush with the inner edge of the bar.
- **System & Continuous Reading (`Inter`):** Powers all descriptive body copy, data grids, inputs, and functional UI metadata to guarantee crisp legibility across varying resolutions.
- **Eyebrow / Overline:** Capitalized, letter-spaced (`0.12em`), set in `Inter` Bold, and rendered in Navy or Burgundy to establish section context before the main serif headline.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy draws on traditional editorial book design: structured, disciplined, and generous in margins.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop:** 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas of `1200px` (or `800px` for formal single-column reading notices). Outer margin defaults to `2.5rem` (`40px`), with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters.
- **Tablet:** 8-column grid with `2rem` (`32px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.
- **Mobile:** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Spacing relies on multiples of `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- Generous paragraph spacing (`space-md` to `space-lg`) prevents dense visual blocks, ensuring critical notices remain effortless to scan and review.
- Header groups are paired with their overlines using `space-xs` and separated from subsequent body blocks by `space-lg` to `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects deep drop shadows and skeuomorphic blurs in favor of flat architectural definition, hairline framing, and intentional surface contrast.

### Planar Structure
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Set to Paper (`#F4F5F8`).
- **Level 1 (Card / Document Layer):** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces defined by a crisp 1px hairline border in Line (`#D3D8E3`). Shadows are entirely absent or limited to a crisp, unblurred 1px hairline displacement (`0 1px 1px rgba(20, 41, 92, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Modals / Overlays):** Solid White surfaces framed by a 1px border (`#D3D8E3`) paired with a neutral backdrop scrim (`rgba(20, 41, 92, 0.3)`).
- **Accent Lines & Anchors:** Depth is communicated horizontally through borders and vertically via 3px or 4px solid vertical bars on the left border of lead modules (Navy for general notices; Burgundy for urgent actions).

## Shapes

Corners are only lightly rounded (6px controls, about 8px panels) so the system stays formal and document-like while feeling less severe.

### Geometric Principles
- **Borders & Corners:** Buttons, inputs, selects and badges use a 6px radius; cards and panels use about 8px. Nothing is fully pill-shaped.
- **Rules & Dividers:** Continuous hairline dividers (`1px` solid `#D3D8E3`) demarcate headers, form sections, and line-item lists.
- **Accent Indicators:** Vertical left border bars must measure strictly `3px` or `4px` in width with sharp edges, running the full vertical height of their parent element or heading group.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Navy (`#14295C`) background, White (`#FFFFFF`) text, 6px radius, height `44px`, horizontal padding `24px`. On hover: `#0E1D42`.
- **Accent / Destructive:** Solid Burgundy (`#7C1022`) background, White (`#FFFFFF`) text. Reserved strictly for final submissions, irrevocable decisions, or explicit urgent actions.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent background, 1px solid Navy (`#14295C`), Navy text. Hover: Navy Tint (`#E3E8F3`).
- **Tertiary / Text:** No border, Navy or Slate text with an underlined text link on hover.

### Notice & Alert Blocks
- **Standard Notice:** White background, 1px hairline border in Line (`#D3D8E3`), anchored by a 4px vertical bar on the left edge in Navy (`#14295C`).
- **Critical / Deadline Alert:** Burgundy Tint (`#F6E4E7`) background, framed by a 1px border in `#E5BAC1` with a 4px solid vertical bar in Burgundy (`#7C1022`). Headline and date highlights use Burgundy text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** White background, 6px radius, 1px solid Line (`#D3D8E3`), height `44px`, padding `12px 16px`. Text in Ink (`#1A2138`), placeholder in Slate (`#4A5268`). Focus state: 1px border in Navy (`#14295C`) with a crisp 1px Navy offset ring (no fuzzy blur).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Square geometry with a 2px radius, 1px border in Slate (`#4A5268`). Checked state fills with Navy (`#14295C`) displaying a sharp, clean white checkmark or center square.

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- **Status Badges:** 6px radius, padding `4px 8px`, `overline` typography.
  - *Informational:* Navy Tint (`#E3E8F3`) background with Navy (`#14295C`) text.
  - *Action Required / Deadline:* Burgundy Tint (`#F6E4E7`) background with Burgundy (`#7C1022`) text.

### Cards & Document Containers
- White background, about 8px radius, framed entirely by a 1px Line (`#D3D8E3`) border. Generous internal padding (`24px` to `32px`). Headers inside cards feature Hanken Grotesk and are separated from body contents by either white space or a subtle 1px divider rule.
